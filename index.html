Entrepreneurship

Entrepreneurship is a system, not a personality trait. A founder or team, an evidence-backed opportunity, a supportive or hostile environment and an organisation that can deliver repeatedly all have to work together.

CO4 · Explain the methodology to start an enterprise systematically

Startup Opportunities and Process

Starting a venture is a sequence of progressively larger commitments. This unit covers how to find and frame the problem (design thinking and brainstorming), where today's opportunities come from (the new industrial revolution), how to turn an idea into a venture and choose its route, and the policy, registration, ecosystem, approval and tax steps that make it legal.

CO5 · Make use of Government initiatives and support organisations for starting new ventures

Government Initiatives and Institutional Support

Support is stage-specific capital and capability, not an entitlement. This unit covers what Central and State governments do, the difference between incentives, subsidies, grants and schemes, inclusive growth, Startup India, the MSME Act and Udyam, Tamil Nadu support channels, and every major source of finance from banks to angels, VC, bootstrapping, crowdfunding and alliances.
