III.1 Nature of a business plan

You should be able to: Explain the purpose and nature of a business plan.

  • 📖 Read first: U21CAX01 Study Material, p. 29
  • ⏱ About 10 minutes to revise

1In a nutshell

A business plan is a written document that explains what the venture will do, who it will serve, how it will operate, how much money it needs, how it will earn revenue and how it will handle risk. It is both a thinking tool for the founder and a communication tool for banks, investors, incubators, partners and evaluators.

  1. Internal job. It helps founders test logic, expose assumptions, coordinate decisions, allocate resources and set milestones.
  2. External job. It helps investors, lenders, partners, suppliers, key employees and sometimes customers judge credibility, risk, resource need and return.
  3. A plan is a living document. Revise it when market feedback, cost estimates, technical assumptions or customer segments change.
  4. Length and detail depend on the decision being requested, but the logic must stay connected: customer, value, channel, forecast, capacity, cost, cash and funding.
  5. Label every statement honestly. An assumption is a belief still to be tested, evidence is something observed or measured, and an aspiration is a target the team hopes to reach.

2What every plan must answer

  • Problem and opportunityWhat pain exists, and for whom?
  • Solution and value propositionWhat is offered, and what outcome does the customer get?
  • Market and segmentWho buys first, and how many such customers are reachable?
  • Business modelHow the venture earns revenue.
  • Operations and resourcesHow it will be produced and delivered.
  • Finance and riskWhat it costs, when cash runs short, and what could go wrong.
Who reads a business plan, and what they look for
ReaderMain question
FounderDoes the logic hold, and what should we do first?
InvestorIs the opportunity large, the team capable and the return worth the risk?
Bank or lenderCan the venture repay on time from its cash flow?
SupplierWill this customer order steadily and pay on time?
Key employee or partnerIs the venture credible enough to commit to?

3See it applied

Example

A startup producing modular assistive devices should plan product variants, user groups, hospitals and NGOs as channels, manufacturing cost, certification needs and service support.

Case lens

WONDRx and CaneBOT

A neat document alone is not a plan. For WONDRx or CaneBOT, the plan must explain the problem, product, customer, delivery model, costs, risks and evidence of demand.

Think: For CaneBOT, name one assumption, one piece of evidence and one aspiration a plan might contain.

4Avoid these mistakes

  • Writing the plan as only a description of the product.
  • Showing financial numbers that are not connected to operational assumptions.
  • Hiding risks instead of acknowledging and mitigating them.

5Key terms

Business plan
A written document that explains a venture's offering, customers, operations, money needs, revenue model and risk handling.
Assumption
A belief the plan relies on that has not yet been confirmed by evidence.
Aspiration
A target the team hopes to achieve, which must not be presented as evidence.

7Exam practice: 2-mark questions

Answer in your own words first, then open the model answer to compare.

Explain how a business plan serves both the entrepreneur and external stakeholders.

For the entrepreneur, the plan is a thinking tool. It tests logic, exposes assumptions, coordinates decisions and sets milestones. For external stakeholders such as investors, lenders, suppliers and partners, it is a communication tool that lets them judge credibility, risk, resource need and return before committing.

Distinguish an assumption, evidence and an aspiration in a business plan.

An assumption is a belief the plan depends on but has not yet tested, for example a 5 percent conversion rate. Evidence is something observed or measured, such as 20 paid pilot orders. An aspiration is a target the team hopes to reach, such as market leadership in three years. A credible plan labels each one honestly.
