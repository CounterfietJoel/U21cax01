III.2 Writing a business plan

You should be able to: Identify major sections of a business plan.

  • 📖 Read first: U21CAX01 Study Material, p. 29
  • ⏱ About 10 minutes to revise

1In a nutshell

A complete plan usually contains an executive summary, company description, problem statement, solution, market analysis, competitor analysis, marketing plan, operations plan, organisation plan, financial plan, implementation schedule, risk analysis and appendices. The executive summary is written last but placed first.

  1. The executive summary states the problem, solution, target market, evidence, business model, advantage, team, headline financial logic, next milestone and the ask, in one or two pages.
  2. Start with the problem and the customer, then explain the offering, the market need and the competition.
  3. Show how money will be earned, with cost, revenue and cash-flow logic.
  4. Close with implementation milestones, responsibilities and risks.
  5. Consistency is the central quality test. Sales in the marketing plan must be deliverable by operations, capacity must be supported by people, suppliers and working capital, and hiring and equipment timing must appear in the cash flow.

2Sections and their decision purpose

  • Executive summaryLets a busy reader decide whether to read further.
  • Problem, solution and marketShows there is a real need and a reachable customer.
  • Marketing planShows how customers will be acquired and kept.
  • Operations planShows the offering can be produced and delivered reliably.
  • Organisation planShows who is responsible for what.
  • Financial planShows the money needed, profitability and cash timing.
  • Implementation and riskShows milestones, owners and responses to what may go wrong.
Consistency checks across sections
If this section saysThen this section must show
Marketing forecasts 5,000 units in year oneOperations has the capacity, suppliers and quality control for 5,000 units
Operations needs two new technicians in month 4Cash flow includes their salaries from month 4
Customers get 60-day creditCash flow shows receipts 60 days after sale, and working capital covers the gap
Growth doubles salesInventory, support and service capacity also grow

3See it applied

Example

For a campus EV charging solution, the plan should include user groups, number of vehicles, charging time, power cost, station location, payment model, maintenance and safety approvals.

Case lens

WONDRx

If WONDRx's marketing section plans clinic sign-ups across a city, its operations section must show onboarding and support staff, and its cash flow must show their cost before clinics start paying.

Think: Name one more cross-section consistency check WONDRx's plan needs.

4Avoid these mistakes

  • Copying generic internet templates blindly.
  • Inflating demand estimates.
  • Leaving implementation responsibilities vague.

5Key terms

Executive summary
A short opening section, written last, that presents the whole venture case and the ask.
Consistency
Agreement among the plan's sections, so that sales, capacity, people, cash and funding support each other.
