II.9 Registration of small scale industry

You should be able to: Explain registration procedures for small-scale industries.

  • 📖 Read first: U21CAX01 Study Material, p. 24
  • ⏱ About 10 minutes to revise

1In a nutshell

Registration gives a small enterprise a formal identity and opens access to schemes, bank support and procurement. Today MSMEs register through Udyam Registration on the official Government of India portal. It is free, online and based on self-declaration.

  1. MSMEs are classified on composite criteria, meaning investment in plant, machinery or equipment and annual turnover. The class depends on both limits.
  2. Limits shown on the Udyam portal (effective 1 April 2025): Micro up to ₹2.5 crore investment and ₹10 crore turnover. Small up to ₹25 crore and ₹100 crore. Medium up to ₹125 crore and ₹500 crore.
  3. Use only the official portal (udyamregistration.gov.in). No agent or private site is authorised, and there is no fee.
  4. Registration is a category, not a single event. Depending on activity, a venture may also need GST, local body permission, pollution control consent, labour registrations, a factory licence, a trademark, an import-export code or sector approvals.
  5. MSME status does not remove any other legal obligation. Verify current limits and procedures before applying.

4Avoid these mistakes

  • Using unofficial or fee-charging registration sites.
  • Thinking MSME status removes other legal obligations.
  • Using old limits without checking the current portal.

5Key terms

Udyam Registration
The official, free, online Government of India registration that gives an eligible enterprise its MSME identity and classification.
MSME
A micro, small or medium enterprise, classified by composite limits on investment and turnover.
Composite criteria
Classification that requires both the investment and the turnover condition to be met.

    Udyam gives MSME identity and classification. It is not startup recognition, a patent or a loan approval.

  2. 2An enterprise has ₹2 crore investment and ₹40 crore turnover. Under the current limits it is

    Investment is within the micro limit, but turnover exceeds ₹10 crore, so composite criteria place it in Small (up to ₹25 crore and ₹100 crore).

7Exam practice: 2-mark questions

Answer in your own words first, then open the model answer to compare.

State the purpose of Udyam Registration.

Udyam Registration gives an eligible micro, small or medium enterprise a formal MSME identity and classification through the free official Government of India portal. That identity enables access to MSME schemes, credit support, procurement preferences and delayed-payment protection.
