V.5 MSME Act and MSME policy in India

You should be able to: Explain the MSME Act and MSME policy in India.

  • 📖 Read first: U21CAX01 Study Material, p. 58
  • ⏱ About 10 minutes to revise

1In a nutshell

MSME policy supports micro, small and medium enterprises through recognition, finance, procurement preference, technology upgrading, cluster development, skills, delayed-payment protection and institutional facilitation. The MSMED Act, 2006 gives the legal framework. Udyam is the current free, online registration route for MSME identity.

  1. Classification uses composite investment and turnover limits (effective 1 April 2025, Udyam portal checked 2 August 2026). Micro is up to ₹2.5 crore investment and ₹10 crore turnover, Small up to ₹25 crore and ₹100 crore, and Medium up to ₹125 crore and ₹500 crore.
  2. Delayed payments (MSMED Act): section 15 caps the agreed payment period at 45 days from acceptance. Section 16 provides compound interest with monthly rests at three times the RBI-notified bank rate on late payment. Sections 18 to 21 cover the MSE Facilitation Councils for disputes.
  3. Udyam does not replace incorporation, tax, local or sector approvals.
  4. Many Indian entrepreneurs begin as MSMEs, not VC-funded startups. MSMEs are central to jobs, manufacturing, services, exports and regional development.
  5. Update MSME status as the enterprise grows, and use only the official Udyam portal.

2MSME essentials

  • MicroLowest investment and turnover limits.
  • SmallLarger scale with higher limits.
  • MediumEstablished scale and significant turnover.
  • UdyamOfficial free online registration.
  • Policy supportCredit, procurement, technology, training and markets.

4Avoid these mistakes

  • Using unofficial agents or portals instead of the official Udyam portal.
  • Classifying on investment alone, when both investment and turnover apply.
  • Not updating MSME status as the enterprise grows.

5Key terms

MSME
Micro, small or medium enterprise, classified by investment in plant and machinery or equipment and by turnover.
Udyam registration
The free, official online registration that gives an enterprise its MSME identity and classification.
MSMED Act, 2006
The law that classifies MSMEs and provides promotion measures and delayed-payment protection.

7Exam practice: 2-mark questions

Answer in your own words first, then open the model answer to compare.

Distinguish DPIIT startup recognition from Udyam MSME registration.

DPIIT recognition is a startup status under Startup India, based on age, turnover, eligible form and innovation, and it opens startup-specific benefits. Udyam registration is MSME identity and classification based on investment and turnover, and it opens MSME benefits such as credit, procurement and delayed-payment protection. Neither is incorporation, and an enterprise may hold both.
