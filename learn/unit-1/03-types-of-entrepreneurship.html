I.3 Types of entrepreneurship

You should be able to: Classify entrepreneurship types with examples.

  • 📖 Read first: U21CAX01 Study Material, p. 7
  • ⏱ About 10 minutes to revise

1In a nutshell

Textbooks usually classify entrepreneurs. The best-known functional classification has four types, which are innovative, imitative (adoptive), Fabian and drone. Entrepreneurs are also grouped by business activity and by motive, and modern discussions add social entrepreneurs, technopreneurs and intrapreneurs.

  1. Innovative entrepreneurs introduce new products, methods, markets or business combinations.
  2. Imitative or adoptive entrepreneurs adopt proven ideas and adapt them to local conditions.
  3. Fabian entrepreneurs are cautious and change only when they have to. Drone entrepreneurs resist change even when the market demands it.
  4. By business activity, the types are manufacturing, trading, service, agricultural, corporate, technical, non-technical, professional, private, state and joint.
  5. By motive, necessity-driven entrepreneurship starts when income alternatives are limited. Opportunity-driven entrepreneurship starts from an attractive chance to create value. Real founders often have both motives.
  6. Modern categories: social entrepreneur (mission with sustainable revenue), technopreneur (technology is the core value driver), intrapreneur (venture inside an existing organisation), scalable startup and small business.

2Functional classification (classic exam answer)

  • InnovativeCreates or commercialises something new.
  • Imitative / adoptiveAdapts a successful model or technology to a new context.
  • FabianSlow, cautious and change-averse until forced.
  • DroneRefuses change and may lose competitiveness.
Necessity-driven versus opportunity-driven entrepreneurship
DimensionNecessity-drivenOpportunity-driven
TriggerLimited acceptable income or employment alternativesA perceived unmet need, innovation or attractive market
First objectiveIncome, continuity and livelihood securityValue creation, independence, growth or impact
Early priorityReliable demand, cash cycle and stabilityValidation, differentiation and scalable economics
Suitable supportCapability, market linkage, basic finance and resilienceIncubation, IP, specialist talent, networks and risk capital

3See it applied

Example

An AI inspection startup is innovative if it builds a new defect-detection model, imitative if it adapts an existing solution for local foundries and Fabian if it delays adopting AI until customers demand it.

Case lens

Greenviro Global, Coimbatore

The same venture can be classified through more than one lens. By function it is innovative, because it creates new value from coconut waste. By activity it is manufacturing, and by mission it has a social and environmental purpose.

Think: Classify Greenviro using two different lenses and justify each in one line.

4Avoid these mistakes

  • Forcing every venture into a single type. Real entrepreneurs can show more than one type over time.
  • Treating the necessity and opportunity labels as a ranking of how good a founder is.
  • Using only the classic four types when a modern category (social, technopreneur, intrapreneur) explains the venture better.

5Key terms

Intrapreneurship
Entrepreneurial venture activity carried out inside an existing organisation.
Social entrepreneurship
Pursuing a measurable social or environmental mission through a financially sustainable operating model.
Necessity-driven entrepreneurship
Entrepreneurship that starts mainly because satisfactory employment or income alternatives are limited.
Opportunity-driven entrepreneurship
Entrepreneurship that starts mainly from an attractive possibility to create value, gain independence, solve a problem or grow.
Technopreneur
An entrepreneur whose venture uses technology as its core value driver.

6Quick check

Pick an answer to see the explanation. Get all of them right to mark this topic as revised.

  1. 1A garment unit owner continues manual cutting even after every competitor and customer has moved to CNC cutting. Which type is this?

    Drone entrepreneurs resist change even when the market clearly demands it. A Fabian entrepreneur would change once forced. This owner refuses even then.

  2. 2An engineer adapts a proven European solar-dryer design for Tamil Nadu farmers. Which type is this?

    Imitative or adoptive entrepreneurs adopt proven ideas and adapt them to local customers, costs and conditions.

  3. 3Which statement about necessity-driven entrepreneurship is correct?

    The label describes the starting condition, not the founder's worth. A necessity venture can become opportunity-led when repeat demand supports growth.

7Exam practice: 2-mark questions

Answer in your own words first, then open the model answer to compare.

State the meaning of intrapreneurship.

Intrapreneurship is entrepreneurial activity carried out inside an existing organisation. An employee (an intrapreneur) develops a new product, process or venture using the organisation's resources, for example an automobile company's internal EV battery venture.

Define social entrepreneurship.

Social entrepreneurship is the pursuit of a measurable social or environmental mission through a financially sustainable operating model, not through charity alone.

Define necessity-driven entrepreneurship.

Necessity-driven entrepreneurship begins mainly when satisfactory employment or income alternatives are limited, so self-employment becomes a practical livelihood route.

State the meaning of opportunity-driven entrepreneurship.

Opportunity-driven entrepreneurship begins mainly when a person identifies an attractive possibility to create value, solve a problem, gain independence or pursue growth.
