I.7 Companies and startups

You should be able to: Differentiate established companies and startups.

  • 📖 Read first: U21CAX01 Study Material, p. 11
  • ⏱ About 10 minutes to revise

1In a nutshell

An established company executes a known business model, with stable customers, processes, departments and metrics. A startup searches for a repeatable and scalable business model under high uncertainty. A startup is not just a smaller company.

  1. Companies optimise operations, but startups test assumptions.
  2. Companies prepare annual plans, but startups run experiments.
  3. Companies reduce variation, but startups learn from variation until they reach product-market fit.
  4. A small business can stay local and stable. A scalable startup aims to grow rapidly through innovation, technology, network effects or scalable delivery.
  5. Corporate entrepreneurship (intrapreneurship) is innovation inside an established organisation.

2Five forms to tell apart

  • Established companyExecutes a validated model.
  • StartupSearches for a validated, repeatable model.
  • Small businessEarns stable local or niche revenue.
  • Scalable startupDesigned for rapid growth and replication.
  • Corporate entrepreneurshipBuilds new ventures inside an existing firm.
Company versus startup
BasisEstablished companyStartup
Business modelKnown and validatedBeing searched for
Main activityExecution and optimisationExperimentation and learning
PlanningAnnual plans and budgetsHypotheses and tests
Attitude to variationReduce itLearn from it until product-market fit
UncertaintyLow to moderateHigh

3See it applied

Example

A local repair shop is a small business. A platform that standardises repair booking across cities is a startup. An automobile company launching an internal EV battery venture is corporate entrepreneurship.

Case lens

Quixy (Vividminds Technologies)

Compare a normal IT service firm with Quixy's no-code platform. The difference is not when they registered. Quixy searches for a repeatable, scalable model that lets revenue grow faster than costs.

Think: What evidence would show that Quixy has found a repeatable model?

4Avoid these mistakes

  • Calling every new company a startup.
  • Pursuing high growth without healthy unit economics.
  • Using startup language to hide weak fundamentals.

5Key terms

Startup
A young venture searching for and developing a repeatable, scalable and sustainable model under uncertainty.
Scalability
The ability to grow revenue or impact faster than the growth of key resource costs.
Product-market fit
The point at which a product clearly satisfies a market, shown by repeated use and willingness to pay.
