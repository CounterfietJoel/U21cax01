IV.7 Registration and legal process of startups

You should be able to: Describe startup registration and legal procedures.

  • 📖 Read first: U21CAX01 Study Material, p. 46
  • ⏱ About 10 minutes to revise

1In a nutshell

Registration starts with choosing a legal structure, usually a private limited company or LLP for scalable startups. After incorporation, an eligible startup may apply for DPIIT recognition. The legal process also covers PAN, a bank account, GST if applicable, labour and shop-and-establishment registration, IP protection, contracts, data protection and sector licences.

  1. Incorporation creates the legal entity and its governance. DPIIT recognition is a separate, later status.
  2. A private limited company (Companies Act 2013) can issue shares, which suits equity investors. An LLP (LLP Act 2008) has lighter compliance but cannot issue equity shares, so VC investors usually prefer companies.
  3. Intellectual property covers patents, industrial designs, copyright, trademarks and trade secrets, plus contracts that define ownership and use.
  4. Practical IP sequence: confirm who owns the contributions (student, employee, institution, collaborator) → document the invention and prior art → do a preliminary search → use confidentiality controls → choose the protection route → file before uncontrolled disclosure → then pitch and run trials with proportionate protection.
  5. Prepare a founder agreement, customer and vendor contracts and IP ownership records. Copied contracts create serious risk.

2Steps

  • Choose structureLiability, investment and compliance differ by form.
  • IncorporateRegister with the relevant authority.
  • Bank and booksOpen a bank account and maintain accounts.
  • RecognitionApply for DPIIT recognition if eligible.
  • ApprovalsGST, labour, local body, pollution, import-export and sector licences.
  • Contracts and IPFounder agreement, customer and vendor contracts, IP records.
IP protection routes
RouteProtectsVenture action
PatentA qualifying inventionConfirm ownership, search prior art, keep it confidential and seek professional filing advice
Industrial designThe visual features of an articleRecord creation and consider filing before publication
CopyrightOriginal expression such as code, text and artRecord authorship, licences and contributor assignment

3See it applied

Example

A health-tech startup may need company incorporation, DPIIT recognition, clinical validation, data privacy safeguards, medical-device regulatory awareness and strong user-consent practices.

Case lens

Startup India Kit, TN Startup Policy and WIPO IP guide

Use them as a process trio. Register and get recognised, understand state support, and protect anything you will disclose publicly before you pitch.

Think: Why should a team file or protect an invention before presenting it at a public pitch event?

4Avoid these mistakes

  • Assuming the legal form does not affect liability, investment or compliance.
  • Treating DPIIT recognition as incorporation.
  • Using copied contracts.
  • Disclosing an invention publicly before deciding how to protect it.

5Key terms

Incorporation
The legal process of forming a company or LLP as a separate legal entity.
Intellectual property
Legally protectable creations of the mind, such as inventions, designs, creative works, brands and trade secrets.
Freedom to operate
Confidence that making and selling a product will not infringe someone else's valid IP.

7Exam practice: 2-mark questions

Answer in your own words first, then open the model answer to compare.

Define incorporation.

Incorporation is the legal process of registering a company or LLP with the relevant authority (the Registrar of Companies through MCA in India), creating a separate legal entity with its own rights, liabilities and perpetual succession.

Define intellectual property.

Intellectual property is a legally protectable creation of the mind, such as an invention (patent), the visual design of an article, original works including code (copyright), a brand (trademark) or confidential know-how (trade secret).
