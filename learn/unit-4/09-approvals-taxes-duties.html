IV.9 Approvals, taxes and duties for new ventures

You should be able to: Identify approvals, taxes and duties payable by new ventures.

  • 📖 Read first: U21CAX01 Study Material, p. 49
  • ⏱ About 10 minutes to revise

1In a nutshell

Approvals depend on the nature, location, legal form and sector of the business. A software firm needs different approvals from a food unit, drone company, factory or medical-device maker. The National Single Window System (NSWS) helps identify, apply for and track many central and state approvals. Taxes and duties may include GST, income tax, TDS or TCS, professional tax in some states, customs duty and sector fees.

  1. Approval categories are separate decisions. Incorporation does not authorise every product, workplace or transaction. Keep an applicability register covering entity, tax, premises, labour, fire and electrical safety, pollution, product standards, sector licences, import-export, consumer duties, data and IP.
  2. A sector-specific approval is a licence or registration required because of the product or activity, such as FSSAI for food or medical-device rules for health products.
  3. Understand invoices, input tax credit, books of accounts and statutory filing dates. Plan tax compliance before sales scale.
  4. Charantimath's setup sequence (Fig. 5.1): select the project → decide the constitution → registration → clearances → land or shed → project report → finance → plant and machinery → implement. Verify current registrations and clearances on official portals.
  5. Review compliance whenever the business expands or changes activity, and consult a qualified professional for filings.

2How to work out approvals

  • IdentifyThe activity and the location.
  • CheckEntity, local body, GST, labour, pollution, factory and sector licences.
  • Use portalsNSWS and state portals to list approvals.
  • Keep recordsTax records, with professional help for filings.
  • ReviewWhenever the business expands or changes.

3See it applied

Example

A food-product startup may need FSSAI registration or licence, GST if applicable, packaging and labelling compliance, a local trade licence and quality records.

Case lens

Autocracy Machinery

A machinery maker needs more than incorporation and Udyam. It also needs factory and premises permissions, power, fire safety, pollution consent, labour registrations, product safety and GST, each checked for its own location and activity.

Think: List three approval categories Autocracy must track that a software startup would not.

4Avoid these mistakes

  • Assuming one certificate covers everything, when approvals are activity-specific.
  • Leaving tax compliance until after sales scale.
  • Giving or following legal advice without verification.

5Key terms

NSWS
National Single Window System, a portal to identify, apply for and track central and state approvals.
GST
Goods and Services Tax, an indirect tax on supply of goods and services, with input tax credit.
Sector-specific approval
A licence or registration required because of the product or activity, beyond general registration.
Applicability register
A venture's own list of which registrations, approvals and duties apply to it, with status and renewal dates.

7Exam practice: 2-mark questions

Answer in your own words first, then open the model answer to compare.

State the meaning of a sector-specific approval.

A sector-specific approval is a licence, registration or clearance required because of the particular product or activity, beyond general entity and tax registration. Examples are FSSAI for food businesses, drug or medical-device approvals for health products and pollution consent for certain manufacturing.
