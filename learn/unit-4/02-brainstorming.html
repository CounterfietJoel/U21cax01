IV.2 Idea generation with brainstorming

You should be able to: Use brainstorming for startup idea generation.

  • 📖 Read first: U21CAX01 Study Material, p. 43
  • ⏱ About 10 minutes to revise

1In a nutshell

Brainstorming is a group method for generating many ideas quickly. Participants postpone criticism, build on each other's ideas and aim for quantity and variety. It needs structure, meaning a clear problem statement, a time limit, diverse participants, visible recording and a separate screening stage.

  1. Ideation is the stage in which the team generates and develops possible solutions.
  2. Divergent thinking widens the options. Convergent thinking narrows them using criteria. Keep the two apart.
  3. Generating ideas silently first, then sharing, stops dominant voices from narrowing the range.
  4. After generating, cluster similar ideas and score them for desirability, feasibility, viability and impact.
  5. Other methods covered in Unit II, such as SCAMPER, mind mapping, interviews and observation, feed the same funnel.

2A structured session

  • FrameWrite the challenge clearly as a 'How might we…' question.
  • Silent startEach person writes ideas alone first.
  • Share and buildRead ideas out, build on others' ideas, and welcome wild ones.
  • ClusterGroup similar ideas.
  • ScoreRate on desirability, feasibility, viability and impact.

3See it applied

Example

For reducing water wastage in hostels, brainstorming may produce sensor taps, leak detection, gamified dashboards, maintenance alerts and greywater reuse.

Case lens

Krishigati

A brainstorm on 'How might we cut weeding labour for a two-acre farmer?' could yield a tool-bar attachment, a shared-rental model, a manual push tool or a cooperative service. Only screening shows which is affordable and usable.

Think: Add two more ideas to Krishigati's list, then pick a criterion to screen them.

4Avoid these mistakes

  • Letting dominant voices reduce idea diversity.
  • Screening too early, which kills creativity.
  • Treating a brainstormed idea as validated.

5Key terms

Brainstorming
A group technique that generates many ideas quickly by deferring judgement.
Ideation
The stage of generating and developing possible solutions.
Divergent thinking
Thinking that widens the range of options.
Convergent thinking
Thinking that narrows options using evidence and criteria.

7Exam practice: 2-mark questions

Answer in your own words first, then open the model answer to compare.

State the meaning of ideation.

Ideation is the stage of design thinking in which a team generates, combines and develops many possible solutions to a defined problem before selecting concepts to prototype.

Define divergent thinking.

Divergent thinking widens the range of possibilities by producing many varied ideas without immediate judgement, as in brainstorming.

Define convergent thinking.

Convergent thinking narrows many options to a few by applying criteria and evidence, such as must-have screens or weighted scoring.
