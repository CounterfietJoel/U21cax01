IV.6 Startup policy and opportunities

You should be able to: Explain startup policies and opportunity areas.

  • 📖 Read first: U21CAX01 Study Material, p. 46
  • ⏱ About 10 minutes to revise

1In a nutshell

Startup policy provides recognition, support, incentives and ecosystem infrastructure for eligible startups. These include tax benefits, procurement relaxation, incubation, seed funding, IP support and mentoring. Policy is an enabler, not a substitute for customer value.

  1. DPIIT recognition criteria cover age, eligible legal form, turnover, originality of the entity and innovation or scalability. As stated in the Course Reader (verified 2 August 2026), eligible forms are a private limited company, registered partnership, LLP or cooperative society. The limits are 10 years and turnover below ₹200 crore, or 20 years and ₹300 crore for DeepTech. Recheck these on the portal before applying.
  2. The entity must not be formed by splitting or reconstructing an existing business.
  3. Screen any policy benefit by eligibility, stage, need, obligations, timing and evidence, not by the headline amount.
  4. Opportunity areas for engineering students include AI tools, manufacturing automation, green energy, agri-tech, drones, construction technology, assistive devices, water, waste, cybersecurity, embedded systems and skilling platforms.
  5. Match the opportunity area to local strengths and founder skills, and use incubators and mentors to interpret schemes correctly.

2Using policy well

  • EnablerPolicy supports value. It does not create it.
  • MatchOpportunity area, local strengths and founder skills.
  • CheckEligibility, documents, compliance and deadlines.
  • InterpretUse incubators and mentors to read schemes correctly.
Scheme-screening matrix
HeadingQuestion to answer
EligibilityDoes our entity, age, turnover, sector and location qualify?
StageIs the scheme meant for idea, prototype, pilot, MSME or scale-up?
NeedDoes it address our actual constraint?
ObligationWhat contribution, milestones, reporting, equity or repayment does it require?
TimingWhen does the window close, and when would money arrive?
EvidenceWhat documents and proof must we submit, and what is the official source date?

3See it applied

Example

A startup working on low-cost machine vision for textile defect detection can connect its technical capability with the industrial demand in Coimbatore and Tiruppur.

Case lens

National Startup Awards sector menu

NSA cases span agritech, machinery, climate tech, healthcare, no-code software, aerospace and sustainability, which gives an entry point for every engineering branch.

Think: Pick the NSA sector closest to your branch and name one problem in it worth exploring.

4Avoid these mistakes

  • Building a venture only to obtain a grant.
  • Relying on old rules, since policies change.
  • Forgetting that scheme benefits require documentation and compliance.

5Key terms

Startup policy
Government measures that recognise and support eligible startups through incentives, infrastructure and simplified compliance.
DPIIT recognition
A startup status granted to an eligible entity under the Startup India framework. It is not incorporation, funding or a licence.

7Exam practice: 2-mark questions

Answer in your own words first, then open the model answer to compare.

State the purpose of DPIIT startup recognition.

DPIIT recognition gives an eligible entity startup status under the Startup India framework. Subject to separate conditions, this can open access to benefits such as self-certification, IPR fast-tracking, Section 80-IAC tax exemption, easier public procurement and easier winding up. It does not guarantee funding or customers.
